Shipping & Payments

Where we deliver

We deliver to cities and towns across Pakistan.

Delivery charges

A delivery fee applies to every order. The exact amount is shown in your cart and at checkout before you place your order.

Delivery time

Orders are usually delivered within 3 to 7 working days, depending on your city. Our team will contact you to confirm your order and delivery details.

Payment methods

  • Cash on Delivery: pay the rider when your order arrives.
  • EasyPaisa and JazzCash: send the total to the account shown at checkout and enter your transaction ID. Your order is confirmed once we verify the payment.
