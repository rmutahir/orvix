Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open checkout, enter your details and choose a payment method. You will see your order number on the confirmation page.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, EasyPaisa and JazzCash.

How does EasyPaisa or JazzCash payment work?

Send the order total to the account shown at checkout, then enter your transaction ID. Your order is confirmed once our team verifies the payment, and a representative will let you know.

How much is delivery?

A delivery fee applies to each order. It is shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay.

How long will delivery take?

Usually 3 to 7 working days, depending on your city.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with your order number and photos. See Returns & Refunds.

Why do you need my email and phone number?

We use them to reach you about your order and delivery.

How can I contact you?

Use the contact page. Your message opens in WhatsApp.
