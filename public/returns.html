Returns & Refunds

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

Change of mind

Please contact us before your order ships and we will do our best to cancel it. Once an item has shipped, returns for change of mind are not guaranteed.

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to your EasyPaisa or JazzCash account, or by bank transfer. We will confirm the details with you directly.

How to reach us

Use our contact page and include your order number.
