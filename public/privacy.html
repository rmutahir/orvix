Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information ORVIX collects when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address, mobile number, city and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Your payment method and, for EasyPaisa or JazzCash, the transaction ID you provide.
  • Any review or message you send us.

How we use it

We use your information to process and deliver your order, verify payments, contact you about your order, and improve our store.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with our suppliers and delivery partners to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

We do not collect or store card details. EasyPaisa and JazzCash payments are made inside your own app, and we only receive the transaction ID you enter.

Storage and security

Order information is kept for as long as needed to run our business and meet legal obligations, and access is limited to authorised staff.

Cookies and local storage

Your browser stores your cart and your light or dark mode choice on your own device. We do not use this to track you across other sites.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information by contacting us through our contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
